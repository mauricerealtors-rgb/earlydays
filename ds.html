Welcome Address From Our Headmistress

Dayspring International Academy is a Christian International school founded in the year 2011 during La Côte d’Ivoire war.
DIA is a sister school of Morning Glory International School in Abidjan which has been providing stellar education to the expatriate community in la Côte d’Ivoire for the past 30 years.

At Dayspring International Academy, we pride ourselves in giving our students the scope of learning and the range of skills they need to be leaders in this global world.
Concurrently, we encourage in them the qualities and values that will enable them to exercise that leadership with wisdom, humility and compassion by changing the status quo.

Martin Luther King Jr, once said, “the function of education is to teach one to think critically and think intensively. Intelligence plus character- that is the goal of true education”. Our goal at DIA is to provide a holistic education for your child in a caring, happy and serene atmosphere. Moreover, we care about the spiritual welfare of our students. In this direction, we instill in our students moral values that will help them thrive throughout their lives.

Our curriculum is meticulously designed to achieve our mission and vision. We offer an international curriculum leading to the obtainment of the International General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE) and the AS/ A Level Certificate.
